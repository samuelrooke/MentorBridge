MentorBridge Take the survey

Prepared interns. Less pressure on your team.

MentorBridge would match students with experienced professionals from their field. The mentor prepares the student before the internship and helps with everyday questions during it, so your supervisor has more time for their own work.

We're students, and this is still an idea

We're a team from The Hague University of Applied Sciences. Before anyone builds MentorBridge, we want to find out if it's really needed.

We'd love to know if your municipality, or another government organisation, would be interested.

How it would work

  1. 1

    A student gets a mentor

    An experienced or recently retired professional from the student's own field.

  2. 2

    The mentor prepares them

    What the work is really like, plus feedback on their CV and interviews. Mentors can also introduce prepared students to organisations like yours.

  3. 3

    The mentor stays involved

    During the internship, the student brings everyday questions to the mentor first.

Your supervisor stays in charge. The mentor is extra help for the intern, not a replacement.

Students pay nothing. The organisation that takes the intern pays a placement fee, and the mentor is paid from that.

What we've heard so far

From our interviews with organisations and our research with students.

Interns come on top of the normal workload

Supervisors don't get lighter work when an intern starts. When they're busy, colleagues step in, and guidance becomes less consistent.

Organisations want interns to add real value

With little time for feedback, it takes longer before an intern can do meaningful work.

Students want someone from the field

All 20 students we asked said they wished they could talk to someone working in their field.

Questions you might have

Is MentorBridge a real service?
Not yet. We're testing whether it's needed before anything gets built. That's why your answer matters, even if it's no.
What are you asking from us?
Six minutes for the survey. If you'd rather talk, reply to our email or write to srooke50@gmail.com
Who would the mentors be?
Experienced professionals from the student's field: people close to retirement, recently retired, or with a few spare hours next to their job.
Is this only for municipalities?
No. We'd like to hear from any government organisation in the Netherlands or Belgium that takes on interns. If you know one that should see this, please pass it on.

Would this help your organisation?

Tell us in the survey. We'd rather hear “no” now than build something nobody needs.
