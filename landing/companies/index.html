Good to know

HR question, or something else? Send us a message.

What does it cost?

Nothing during our first version. MentorBridge is free for everybody while we start. We'll decide on a payment model later, and we'll tell you before anything changes.

How many mentors do we need to provide?

One or two to start. You can also take part without providing mentors, and we match your interns with a mentor from your sector.

Does the mentor replace the intern's supervisor?

No. The mentor is an extra source of guidance next to the supervisor.

Can we use it under our own name?

On request. Send us a message and tell us what you have in mind.

What happens with our details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. Read the privacy statement.
