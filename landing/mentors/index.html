Questions and answers

Something else you want to ask? Send us a message.

Can I mentor next to a full-time job?

Yes. MentorBridge is made for people with some free time next to their work. The sessions are short and planned around your week.

How much time does it take?

You decide. When you join the waiting list, you tell us how many hours a month you have and when you're available.

Does it cost anything, or do I get paid?

Joining is free. MentorBridge is free for everybody during our first version, and there is no payment for mentors at the moment.

Online or in person?

Both are possible. You agree with your student what works for you.

What happens with my details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. Read the privacy statement.
