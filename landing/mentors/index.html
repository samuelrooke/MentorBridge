Who can become a mentor

Many people wonder what to do after retirement. Mentoring keeps you close to your field, and a student gets the start you wish you'd had.

Retired or about to retire

Your know-how doesn't have to stop when your job does. Pass it on to someone just starting out.

55+ and not working right now

Between jobs or taking a break? Your experience still counts, and students need it.

Still working, with time to spare

A few free hours a month is enough. You're welcome too.

Questions and answers

Something else you want to ask? Send us a message.

Do I need to be retired?

No. MentorBridge is made for people who are retired or about to retire, and for people aged 55 and over who aren't working right now. If you're still working and have a few hours to spare, you're welcome too.

How much time does it take?

You decide. When you join the waiting list, you tell us how many hours a month you have and when you're available.

Does it cost anything, or do I get paid?

Joining is free. MentorBridge is free for everybody during our first version, and there is no payment for mentors at the moment.

Online or in person?

Both are possible. You agree with your student what works for you.

What happens with my details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. Read the privacy statement.
