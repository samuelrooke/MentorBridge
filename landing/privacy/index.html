Privacy statement

MentorBridge is a student project at The Hague University of Applied Sciences. This page explains what we store about you, why, and how to have it removed.

Your MentorBridge account

When you create an account, we store your first name, surname and email address, and for companies the company name. Your password is stored encrypted; we can't read it. In your profile you can add answers that help us match you, a short introduction and a photo.

When you're matched, we store the sessions you plan and the messages you send. Only you, your match and the MentorBridge team can read your messages. The team can also write in your conversation, always as MentorBridge team. A company only sees a match after the student has agreed to it, and never sees messages.

We also store the first page you visited, the website that sent you (only its name) and the campaign tags in the link you used, if any.

The waiting list and the contact form

When you joined the waiting list, we stored what you filled in:

  • Everyone: your name, email address and sector (optional for mentors).
  • Students: what you want help with, when you're available, and the optional follow-up answers.
  • Mentors: your age range, how many hours a month you have and when you're available.
  • Companies: the company name, your role, and optionally its size and how you'd like to use MentorBridge.

We also store when you signed up and gave consent, the first page you visited, the website that sent you (only its name, like linkedin.com) and the campaign tags in the link you used, if any. When you send a message through the contact form, we store your name, email address and message.

Cookies and visit counting

We don't store your IP address with your sign-up. We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across websites.

Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, only runs after you click Accept in the cookie bar. It sets cookies and sends data about your visit to Google, but not the details you fill in. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the page.

Why

To run your account, to match students and mentors, to contact you, and to count how many people sign up per group and where they found us. We only use your details for MentorBridge, and we never sell them.

We process account details because you need them to use MentorBridge. We process waiting list details because you gave consent when you signed up. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who can see it

The four students in the MentorBridge team. When you're matched, your match also sees your name, introduction, photo and profile answers. The details are stored in a Supabase database, and the website runs on Vercel. If you accept analytics cookies, Google receives data about your visit.

How long we keep it

We delete all details when the project ends.

See, change or remove your details

You can change your profile yourself on the platform. For anything else, send us a message through the contact form and tell us what you'd like. We'll reply by email. You can also email us at mentorbridge.nl@outlook.com.

If you're not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit in Belgium.
