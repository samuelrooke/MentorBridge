Privacy statement

MentorBridge is a student project at The Hague University of Applied Sciences. This page explains what we store about you, why, and how to have it removed.

What we store

When you join the waiting list, we store what you fill in:

  • Everyone: your name, email address and sector (optional for mentors).
  • Students: what you want help with, when you're available, and the optional follow-up answers.
  • Mentors: your age range, how many hours a month you have and when you're available.
  • Companies: the company name, your role, and optionally its size and how you'd like to use MentorBridge.

We also store when you signed up and gave consent, the first page you visited, the website that sent you (only its name, like linkedin.com) and the campaign tags in the link you used, if any. When you send a message through the contact form, we store your name, email address and message.

Cookies and visit counting

We don't store your IP address with your sign-up. We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across websites.

Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, only runs after you click Accept in the cookie bar. It sets cookies and sends data about your visit to Google, but not the details you fill in. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the page.

Why

To contact you about the waiting list, to match students and mentors later, and to count how many people sign up per group and where they found us. We only use your details for MentorBridge, and we never sell them.

We process your details because you gave consent when you signed up. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who can see it

Only the four students in the MentorBridge team. The details are stored in a Supabase database, and the website runs on Vercel. If you accept analytics cookies, Google receives data about your visit.

How long we keep it

We delete all details when the project ends.

See, change or remove your details

Send us a message through the contact form and tell us what you'd like. We'll reply by email. You can also email us at mentorbridge.nl@outlook.com.

If you're not happy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands or the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit in Belgium.
