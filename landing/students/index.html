Good to know

Something else you want to ask? Send us a message.

Is it really free?

Yes. MentorBridge is free for everybody during our first version. We'll decide on a payment model later, and we'll tell you before anything changes.

Who are the mentors?

Experienced professionals from your field. Many of them are retired or about to retire, and want to pass on what they know. We match you by hand, based on your sector and when you're available.

How much time does it take?

A few short sessions with your mentor, online or in person, planned around your schedule.

Can you guarantee me an internship?

No. Nobody can promise that. Your mentor helps you understand what to improve and prepare well, which gives you a better chance.

What happens with my details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. Read the privacy statement.
