Good to know

Something else you want to ask? Send us a message.

What does it cost?

Nothing. MentorBridge is free for students, mentors and organizations during our first version. We'll decide on a payment model later, and we'll tell you before anything changes.

Who are the mentors?

Experienced professionals who are retired or about to retire, people aged 55 and over who aren't working right now, and working professionals with a few hours to spare.

Where does MentorBridge work?

We start in the Netherlands and Belgium.

What happens with my details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. Read the privacy statement.
