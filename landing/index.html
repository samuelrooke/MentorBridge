Questions

Good to know

Something else you want to ask? Send us a message.

Is it really free for students?

Yes. Students never pay. The plan is that organizations that take on interns pay for MentorBridge.

Who are the mentors?

Experienced professionals from your field who have some free time next to their job. We match you by hand, based on your field and what you want help with.

How much time does it take?

A few short sessions with your mentor, online or in person, planned around your schedule.

Can you guarantee me an internship?

No. Nobody can promise that. Your mentor helps you understand what to improve, apply with more confidence and prepare well, which gives you a better chance.

What happens with my details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. MentorBridge is a student project at The Hague University of Applied Sciences.
