Questions and answers

Something else you want to ask? Send us a message.

What does it cost?

Nothing. MentorBridge is free for students, mentors and companies during our first version. We'll decide on a payment model later, and we'll tell you before anything changes.

Who are the mentors?

Experienced professionals who have some free time next to their work and want to pass on what they know.

Where does MentorBridge work?

We start in the Netherlands and Belgium.

What happens with my details?

We only use them to contact you about MentorBridge, and we delete them when the project ends. Read the privacy statement.
